# Words

The site is written by the people who run the space, to people who might come in. Plain sentences, specific nouns, numbers that are true. Copy that sounds like an ad gives a site away faster than any color.

## Where the words come from

- The people who run the space. Ask how they'd explain a workshop to someone at the door, and keep their phrasing where it works.
- Facts over adjectives. "Up to 8 people, 1–4pm on a Saturday" says more than "an intimate hands-on experience".
- Where the real words aren't written yet, the sample content uses plain lorem ipsum. No bracketed notes, no half-real sentences.

## Tone

Friendly and direct, like someone who animates explaining it to a neighbor. Confident about the craft without lecturing. Nobody needs stop motion explained to them in the abstract: say what happens in the room.

## Tells to avoid

- Groups of three: "Sculpt. Shoot. Share." Use one, two or four.
- Every sentence the same length.
- "Not just a studio, it's…" and every other not-X-but-Y.
- Paragraphs that end by restating themselves; pages that close on a neat summary.
- Opening with a rhetorical question.
- An em dash in every paragraph. Commas, full stops, colons and parentheses first.
- A bold lead-in on every bullet, emoji, a heading over every two paragraphs.
- Even-handedness. People have favorites, and saying so is more useful.

## Words to watch

Treat these as a smoke alarm, not a recipe.

- General: delve, tapestry, testament, elevate, seamless, vibrant, nestled, boasts, embark, journey, curated, crafted, meticulous, unforgettable, unparalleled, immerse, indulge, discover, unlock, look no further, whether you're X or Y, more than just, in the heart of, where X meets Y, a celebration of, step into, passionate about.
- Makers spaces: unleash your creativity, bring your ideas to life, maker community, hands-on experience, creative hub, space to create, for all skill levels.
- Animation: bring it to life, the magic of stop motion, frame by frame (as a slogan), every frame tells a story, lights camera action.
- Allowed on purpose: clay, puppets, armatures, frames, sets, the bridge, St Johns.

## Small copy is still copy

Labels are nouns or short imperatives in sentence case. Never "Get started", "Learn more" or "Explore".

| Where | Words |
| --- | --- |
| A form sent | "Got it, thanks. We'll write back soon." |
| A form failed | "That didn't go through. Try again, or write to …" |
| Mailing list sign-up | "You're on the list. Thanks." |
| No workshop dates | "No dates yet. Join the mailing list to hear when there are." |
| Empty notice board | "Nothing on the board right now." |
| 404 | "Nothing here." |

## The last check

Read it out loud. If it sounds like an ad or a LinkedIn post, rewrite it. If it sounds like someone at the space talking across the bench, it's close.
