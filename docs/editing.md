# Editing the site

Go to the site's address with `/edit` on the end and log in. That's where you change everything on the site that changes from week to week.

Two things to know first. Nothing you do there can break the site. And nothing goes live until you press Publish, so you can type, leave and come back.

## Adding dates to a workshop

This is the one you'll do most.

1. Choose Workshops on the left, then the workshop.
2. Under Dates, press Add item.
3. Pick the date, type the time (like "1–4pm"), and if you want, how many places are left.
4. If people book somewhere else, paste that link into Sign-up link. If you leave it empty, the site asks people to write to you.
5. Press Publish.

When a date is full, open it and set Status to Full. Past dates drop off the site on their own, so there's no need to delete them.

## A new workshop

Workshops, then the new-document button at the top of the list. Fill in the name, press Generate next to Web address, add a date or two, and Publish. Everything else is optional and only shows on the site when it's filled in.

To take a workshop off the site without losing it, turn off On the list.

## Events

Anything on the calendar that isn't a workshop: open studio nights, screenings, an open house. Same as workshops: a name, one or more dates, Publish.

## The notice board

Notice board, then the new-document button. Add a headline, the notice, and how to reply. Set Take down on and it disappears on that day by itself.

## The space

The name, the line under it, the status line on the home page ("Opening soon."), the address, hours, email and Instagram. Fill in the address and hours when they're settled. Until then the site says the address is still to come and that you're not open yet.

The home page photo goes here too. Until there is one, the home page shows an empty box with a cross through it.

## Pages

The words at the top of each page, and its line on the home page's contents list. Turning off Page is on takes a page out of the menu until it's ready.

The equipment list on the Studio time page is under Pages, Studio time, List. One row per thing.

## Photos

Every photo asks what's in it. That's for people who can't see it, and the site won't publish a photo without it. A sentence is plenty: "Two clay figures on a tabletop set."

## When something you published doesn't show

Wait a minute and reload. If it's still not there, check that you pressed Publish and not only typed.

## Messages from the forms

Room rental questions, messages and mailing list sign-ups go to Netlify, and Netlify emails each one to the address it's been given. The full lists are in Netlify under Forms.
